## What it does and does not do

- **It does not host or distribute lyrics.** The extension contains no lyrics.
  It asks third-party databases for lyrics at runtime and displays what they
  return, on the user's own device.
- **It has no server.** There is no backend, no proxy, and no shared cache.
  Nothing is collected, transmitted to the authors, or sold.
- **It does not circumvent technological protection measures.** It performs
  ordinary HTTP requests, the same a browser would make.
- **It does not bypass ads or paid tiers.** It is a lyrics panel.
